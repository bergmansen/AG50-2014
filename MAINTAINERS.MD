## 4. Patch Review Responsibilities

Maintainers are responsible for:

- Reviewing submitted patches.
- Checking technical correctness.
- Ensuring documentation quality.
- Identifying compatibility problems.
- Requesting modifications when necessary.
- Approving or rejecting proposed changes.
- Maintaining consistency across subsystems.

## 5. Patch Submission

All contributions should follow the procedures documented in `CONTRIBUTING.MD`.

Patches should be submitted through the official development mailing list.

## 6. Maintainer Authority

Maintainers may:

- Review and discuss proposed changes.
- Request revisions.
- Approve changes within their assigned responsibilities.
- Reject changes that do not meet project requirements.
- Recommend changes to project architecture.

Final project-wide decisions are made by the Project Lead or an explicitly delegated authority.

## 7. Becoming a Maintainer

Maintainer appointments are based on:

- Demonstrated technical knowledge.
- Consistent contributions.
- Understanding of project architecture.
- Ability to review and validate changes.
- Long-term commitment to the project.
