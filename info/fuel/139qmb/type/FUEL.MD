## Fuel Type and Fuel Recommendations

The recommended fuel for the 139QMB engine is **E5 petrol with a 98-octane rating (98 RON)**. This fuel is preferred because it has a lower ethanol content than E10 petrol, making it a better choice for older engines and fuel systems that may contain ethanol-sensitive materials.

Ethanol can have a negative effect on certain rubber and plastic components inside the fuel system. With prolonged exposure to high ethanol concentrations, materials such as fuel hoses, seals, gaskets and other rubber components can deteriorate, harden, swell or crack. This may eventually lead to fuel leaks, poor engine performance or starting problems.

For this reason, using E5 petrol is recommended to help protect the fuel system and maintain the engine's reliability over time. The 98-octane rating also provides a suitable high-octane fuel option, although a higher octane rating does not automatically mean better performance or more power.

### Fuel Recommendations by Region

- **Europe:** Use E5 98 RON petrol where available. Avoid E10 when possible, especially if the fuel-system components are not confirmed to be ethanol-compatible.
- **United States:** Look for premium gasoline with a low ethanol content, preferably ethanol-free gasoline or fuel containing no more than 5% ethanol (E5 equivalent). Check the octane rating carefully, as American AKI octane numbers use a different measurement system from European RON ratings.

### Important Note

Always check the fuel requirements in the official engine, for the specific model year and engine version. Fuel compatibility is particularly important for small carbureted engines, where deteriorating fuel-system components can cause problems with fuel delivery and engine operation.

**Overall recommendation:** E5 98 RON is the preferred fuel choice for this engine to help reduce ethanol-related deterioration of fuel-system components and support long-term reliability.
