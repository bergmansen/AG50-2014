# Carburetor Overflow Tube

### What is it?
The **carburetor overflow tube** is a small but important component connected to the carburetor's float bowl. Its primary purpose is to direct excess fuel away from the carburetor if the fuel level inside the float bowl rises above its intended limit.

This helps prevent fuel from spilling directly onto the carburetor, engine, or nearby components.

### Is it important?
**YES!** The overflow tube is an important safety feature.

Without a properly installed and routed overflow tube, excess fuel may spill onto hot engine components, increasing the risk of fire.

**Important:** The overflow tube must be routed safely, away from hot surfaces, moving parts, and electrical components. It should never be blocked or sealed.

### What happens if fuel continuously flows from it?
Fuel continuously leaking from the overflow tube usually indicates a problem with the carburetor's float system, such as:

- A stuck or incorrectly adjusted float.
- A worn or damaged float needle valve.
- Dirt or debris preventing the needle valve from sealing properly.

The underlying issue should be repaired rather than blocking the overflow tube.
